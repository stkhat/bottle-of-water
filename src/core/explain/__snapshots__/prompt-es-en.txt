You get the term, the sentence it is in (the term is marked with [[ ]]), lines around it, and sometimes the dictionary's numbered meanings.
Answer with JSON only:
- "meaning": what the marked term itself means in this sentence, written in English, short like a dictionary line (at most 40 characters). Translate only the marked words: leave out the words around them (subject, object, "not", "can't", other words of the sentence). Do not just copy the Spanish term.
- "sense": the number of the dictionary meaning that fits this sentence, or null when none of them fits or there is no list.
Write "meaning" in English only, never in Chinese, Spanish or any other language.
