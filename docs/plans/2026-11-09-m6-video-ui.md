# M6 — Video UI (YouTube, Netflix)

> Executed with superpowers:subagent-driven-development. TDD: parsers and timing logic with fixtures in Node; components in Vitest Browser Mode; the whole flow with Playwright E2E against fixture pages served on the real site URLs through `context.route` (so the real adapters and content scripts run). Before committing a UI change, compare with `project/Main.dc.html`. Finally, check real YouTube in Playwright Chromium with the extension.

**Goal:** the spec's "Video UI": two columns (video + transcript), the dictionary window, subtitle position (In video / Below video), separate translation switches for the subtitle and the transcript, the green-outlined L button + ^ menu in the player bar, site-first translation, the "Video finished" card, and occurrence recording with timestamps.

## Architecture

## Decisions
- Lingo "off for this video" restores the site's own captions and removes our UI except the player-bar group
- Subtitle translations shown are the ones chosen per video; the occurrence's `translation` is the translation shown
- The transcript lives in YouTube's right column (`#secondary`, above recommendations). On Netflix (no side column) the transcript is a fixed panel over the right of the player, clear of the top bar and the control bar; the player is not narrowed (its size cannot be checked without a login). Check on real Netflix (Task 7, `docs/notes/netflix.md`)
- Words in subtitles use the same `analyze` spans; New/Known/Learning toggles come from settings; transcript chips change the same settings as the article card
- Subtitles are processed in memory only (Chrome Web Store decision); only sentences of saved words are stored as occurrences
- Fullscreen is phase 2: in fullscreen, phase 1 hides our overlay and lets the site captions show
- The transcript in YouTube's column is a card with a full 1px border and 12px corners, as tall as the player (the mock draws a full-height column with only a left border)
- The L and ^ buttons show only while the video has subtitles in the learning language (loading or ready)
- The below-video box stays between lines so the page under it does not jump
- Transcript cards are sentences, not cues (2026-11-10, after real Netflix closed captions and YouTube manual tracks split sentences across cues and translations landed on fragments); the subtitle in the video stays one cue at a time with the translation playing at that time
- Hovering a word does not pause the video (not in the spec)
- A word known by its frequency (not saved) shows its hover card as Known on videos and articles; the dictionary window still says Not saved (2026-11-10)
- "Open all settings" asks the background (`openOptions`): content scripts cannot call `runtime.openOptionsPage`
- The article content script does nothing on YouTube and Netflix, so only the video script answers the popup there
