# M8c — Sentence history (past occurrences of unsaved words)

> Executed with superpowers:subagent-driven-development. TDD; IndexedDB code in Vitest Browser Mode (real IndexedDB, a fresh database per test as in `term-store.browser.test.ts`), handlers in Node with `fakeBrowser`, UI in Browser Mode, the built extension with Playwright E2E.

**Goal:** Lingo keeps the sentences of pages and videos opened with it on, on the device, so the dictionary window shows "Seen elsewhere · N" for unsaved words too, and saving a word brings those sentences into its history. Settings gets a "Sentence history" section. Screens: mock `Phase 2 — past occurrences of unsaved words` (`project/PastOccurrences.dc.html`).

## Decisions
- Videos keep **played lines only** (decided 2026-11-10); articles keep the sentences of the blocks Lingo analyzes (the visible part of the page and what is scrolled to)
- Never synced, never exported (`exportJson` unchanged). Cap 50 MB (counted by Lingo itself: UTF-16 size of stored strings plus a fixed per-record overhead); when over the cap, whole pages are removed oldest first (`lastSeen`) until the total is under 90 % of the cap
- Not kept: when `settings.sentenceHistory` is off; for hosts in `neverRunOn` or `disabledSites` (checked in the background against the source URL, on top of content scripts not running there). Adding a host to `neverRunOn` removes its kept pages. Turning the switch off stops recording and keeps what is there (Clear removes it)
- Index: each sentence is indexed by term ids (`lang:key`): every base form of each word except the 300 most common, plus the keys of phrases the classifier finds in it (detected and saved). The background computes them from the text (content scripts send only sentences)
- "Seen elsewhere" for an unsaved term: kept sentences with its id, other sources than the current one, newest first, at most 50. For a saved term: its occurrences, as now
- Saving a term (`saveTerm`, not "save all") copies its kept sentences into its occurrences (`origin: "encountered"`, sources upserted from the kept pages), within `MAX_OCCURRENCES`
- Row meta follows the mock: page "host · Nov 8", video "YouTube · at 3:10 · Nov 8" (date of `seenAt`, `shortDate`), for saved terms too

## Tasks
1. **History store** — `src/background/db.ts` Dexie `version(2)`: `historyPages: "id, lastSeen, host"` (`{id, platform, url, host, title, lang, firstSeen, lastSeen, bytes}`, id = `sourceId(platform, normalizedUrl)`), `historySentences: "++id, pageId, &[pageId+text], *keys"` (`{pageId, text, translation?, pos, keys, seenAt}`); `meta` gains `historyBytes`. New `src/background/history-store.ts`: `record(source, items: {text, translation?, pos, keys}[])` (idempotent on `[pageId+text]`, updates `lastSeen`/`title`/bytes, then evicts), `find(termId, {excludeUrl, limit})` → sentences joined with their page, `importInto`-ready shape, `status()` → `{bytes, cap, pages}`, `clear()`, `forgetHost(host)` (subdomains too, like `onHost`). Tests: v1 → v2 upgrade keeps terms; idempotency; byte accounting; eviction order; find order/exclusion/limit; clear; forgetHost
2. **Background** — protocol + client (`REPEATABLE`) + handlers: `recordHistory {source, sentences: {text, translation?, pos}[]}` (gates above; keys from `resources.pack(lang)` lemmas/rank and `classifier(lang)` phrase spans), `historyStatus`, `clearHistory`. `lookup`: `elsewhere` for unsaved terms from history. `saveTerm` imports kept sentences (term-store gets the rows from the handler or a store method; keep the term-store transaction small). The background watches settings and calls `forgetHost` for hosts newly added to `neverRunOn`. Unit tests in `handlers.test.ts` (fake stores) + a Browser Mode test of the import with real stores
3. **Capture and window** — `src/core/text/sentences.ts`: `splitSentences(text, lang)` (`Intl.Segmenter` sentence granularity, `collapseSpaces`; `src/content/article/sentence.ts` `sentenceAt` reuses it). Article (`src/content/article/article.ts`): after each analyzed batch send `recordHistory` with the batch's sentences (`pos: {textQuote}`), once per sentence per page view. Video (`src/content/video/session.ts`): in `played(w, i)` send the card (`flat(cue.text)`, translation when shown, `pos: {timeMs: cue.startMs}`), once per card per watch. Expected failures (stopped service worker, history off) must not log at warn/error. `ArticleUi.tsx` / `VideoUi.tsx`: stop forcing `elsewhere = null` for New; `dict.notSavedNote` only when an unsaved term has no rows. `src/ui/lookup/elsewhere.ts`: meta with the date. Tests: sentences unit tests; article/session browser tests with the test background (sentences sent, played-only for video, nothing when blocked); DictionaryWindow test for an unsaved term with rows
4. **Settings section and E2E** — `src/entrypoints/options/SettingsPage.tsx`: section `history` ("Sentence history", nav entry, placed after Sites) exactly as the mock: lead copy, "Keep sentence history" switch (`settings.sentenceHistory`) with "Never synced. Pages on your Never run on list are not kept.", "Space used" with "N MB of 50 MB" and the bar, "N pages and videos · the oldest are removed first when space runs out" / "Nothing kept yet", "Clear history" row with "Saved words and their sources stay." and a Clear button (refreshes status). MB formatter in `src/ui/format/format.ts`. Strings in `en.json`. Browser Mode tests. E2E (`e2e/`): open article A then article B (both fixtures containing an unsaved word), the dictionary window on B shows "Seen elsewhere · 1" with A's sentence; saving the word keeps it in its history (word list shows the occurrence); Settings shows pages > 0, Clear → "Nothing kept yet"; with the switch off nothing is recorded. `e2e/extension-errors.spec.ts` stays clean. Screenshot pairs against the mock (dictionary window for an unsaved word with history; the Settings section)
