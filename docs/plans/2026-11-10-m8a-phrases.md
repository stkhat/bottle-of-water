# M8a — Dictionary phrase detection

> Executed with superpowers:subagent-driven-development. TDD; data scripts tested on fixtures in Node, the classifier and dictionary in Node, UI in Vitest Browser Mode, the built extension with Playwright E2E.

**Goal:** detect dictionary phrases (idioms, phrasal verbs, prepositional phrases) in pages and subtitles without the user saving them first, and give every detected phrase a meaning and IPA. Phrase meanings come from EJDict, then Wiktionary's Japanese translations, then Wiktionary's English definition (decided 2026-11-10). The built-in translator stays only for selected phrases that no dictionary has.

## Measured (kaikki English dump of 2026-11-09)
327,359 multi-word English entries. With every word in the vocabulary and no placeholder words (one's, someone, something, oneself, somebody, sb, sth): 31,758, of which 6,818 have an `idiomatic` sense, 4,979 are in a "phrasal verbs" category, 2,219 are pos `phrase` and 2,070 `prep_phrase`. Only 59 have a top-level Japanese translation. Glosses for all 31,758 take about 3 MB.

## Phrase list rule
A phrase key is its words lowercased and joined by single spaces (the same key as a saved phrase). The list is the union of:
- EJDict multi-word headwords whose words are all in the vocabulary and that contain no placeholder word
- kaikki entries (`lang_code` en, headword with a space) whose words are all in the vocabulary, with no placeholder word, and with at least one *usable* sense that is tagged `idiomatic`, is in a category whose name contains "phrasal verbs", or belongs to an entry of pos `phrase` or `prep_phrase`. A usable sense has glosses and is not `form-of` / `alt-of` / obsolete / archaic / misspelling (so "thrown away" — past participle of throw away — is left out; "throw away" stays)

Words must match `^[a-z][a-z']*$` after lowercasing (no hyphens, digits or punctuation inside a phrase key).

## Output files
| File | Shape | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `public/packs/en/phrases.json` | `string[]` | the phrase list, sorted by code unit |
| `public/packs/en-defs/dict-<n>.json` + `index.json` | `{ [phrase]: [short, [pos, text][]] }` | English definitions, only for phrases EJDict and the Japanese translations lack. Up to 4 usable senses; `text` = the sense's last gloss; `pos` = `posLabel(entry.pos)`; senses of several entries with the same headword merged in file order. `short` = the first sense's text cut at the first `;`, without a final period. Sharded with `shardDict` (≤ 2,000,000 bytes per shard) |
| `public/packs/en-ja/phrases.json` | `{ [phrase]: [short, senses[]] }` | kaikki Japanese translations (`translations[]` and `senses[].translations[]` with `lang_code: "ja"`, `word` field) for phrases EJDict lacks; `short` = first translation, senses = distinct translations |
