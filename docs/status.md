## Known issues and open points

### Video
- Netflix's Japanese lines are loosely timed, so a few land on a neighboring transcript card; where a site's translation is cut finer than the cards and timed loosely, a short card left without its own line can now show its neighbour's (rule G); sound labels inside a cue stay in the card ("… ( chuckles )")
- Transcript sidebar in real fullscreen not checked in the user's Chrome: Chrome refuses fullscreen to Claude in Chrome's clicks ("not granted"); the narrowing itself was seen working on YouTube in fullscreen during the investigation, and browser tests cover real fullscreen on the fixture pages
- Netflix: the ^ menu goes away with Netflix's control bar when the pointer stops (it shows again with the bar)
- YouTube: real-site layout in theater mode and the narrow single-column layout not checked
- YouTube Shorts: ASR lines are split for the watch page's width (up to 60 characters), so a long one wraps into two or three lines over the narrow video; the dictionary window covers the comments panel while that is open
- YouTube: after using L or ^ the focus stays on Langle's buttons, whose event isolation keeps YouTube's shortcuts (f, k) from reaching the player until the video is clicked
- YouTube ASR: where the English has no punctuation, a card boundary can be 1–3 words off (YouTube's own translation drifts). Where YouTube translates one English sentence as two, the card holds both, or the English is split at a comma. A card split for length (over 30 words) cuts its translation at the clause end said nearest the split; Japanese word order puts some clauses on the other part ("DDR5メモリの現在の価格を考えると、" under the English before it), and 7 of 199 split cards in the corpus show the whole translation on each part (no clause end there). A card cut at a speaker change ">>" cuts its translation the same way; 2% of round 2's cards show no translation (mostly sound labels such as "[laughter]" before a ">>", some short replies whose translation YouTube put after the change)
- Manual tracks: a card never splits a cue, so where the track's sentences end inside its cues a card ends at a cue's end instead: 10–20% of YouTube cards end mid-sentence with the next going on in lower case (Netflix 0.2%). With YouTube's translation, 0.1–0.4% of whole-sentence cards show a Japanese cut mid-sentence (was 11%)
- YouTube Shorts: once, a full load with the user's caption language set to Japanese made no caption request at all, also not after Langle asked the player for the English track ([notes](notes/youtube-shorts.md)); not seen again. Possibly the class fixed on 2026-11-19 (a track already on gets no request when set again)
- Subtitles: the outline of the term whose dictionary window is open goes around the clicked part of a gap phrase only
- Video lines that play while the language data is missing are classified later but not kept in the sentence history

### Meaning in context
- In YouTube comments the lines around the sentence are the comment's other sentences and the replies YouTube has loaded: a one-sentence comment with its replies closed is explained from its sentence alone
- 2026-11-16: the reading placeholder says "Thinking…", and "Starting on-device AI…" only while the model itself has been starting for more than 1 s (the page's base session being created); the card's footer reads "On-device AI" without a line count. The slow start is covered by tests only: it cannot be brought about on demand in the user's Chrome
- Deferred (user, 2026-11-16): hiding the first explanation's delay (about 10 s after the model was idle, nearly all of it the base session's `create()`). Two ways, neither built now: create the page's base session ahead of time when a page loads (costs memory and power on every page where no explanation is asked), or share one base session across pages from an offscreen document (it runs prompts as fast as a content script, M0 check C2, but it cannot start a download, which needs a click on the page)
- Explanations dropped (user, 2026-11-16, [notes](notes/meaning-in-context.md) "When to show an explanation"): the card shows the meaning alone, and the prompt asks for the marked words only. ### YouTube comments
- Short comments ("lol", "wow") are often not decided by the language detector and count as the learning language
- A comment cut off by "Read more" is analyzed whole; its hidden part gets highlights once expanded

### Articles
- A word saved in another tab shows on an open page only after it is classified again (reload or a known-level change)
- Wikipedia sentences keep citation brackets ("5] …", "…[") in "On this page" and the history
- A page whose first analysis ends just as the language pair changes can log "No dictionary from en to en": `warmDictionary` pairs the page's language with the new native language (a rare E2E flake in `extension-errors.spec.ts`)
- On an article or video whose language pack is there but whose dictionary is not (still downloading, or failed), hovering a word shows nothing and logs nothing; since 2026-11-18 the hover card (and a window clicked meanwhile) shows once the data is there, while the pointer is still on the word. If the lookup's "data missing" answer comes back after the data became ready, it still waits for the next change (the pack wait's guard against asking again on the same states)

### Popup, word list, history, sync
- Popup: "On this site" reflects only `disabledSites` (a "Never run on" host still shows the switch on); relative times read "2 minutes ago" where the mock says "2 min ago"
