Which language pairs have enough dictionary data?

Rows are the learning language, columns the native language. The bar is the quality gate: human-made sources give a native meaning for at least 95% of the learning language's 5,000 most frequent content words. The English row (and Spanish → English) is measured; the other cells are estimated from source sizes.
