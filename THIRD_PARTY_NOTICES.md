# Third-party notices

Langle uses data derived from the sources below. The files are generated by `npm run data:build`, `npm run data:es` and `npm run data:native` (scripts in `scripts/data/`). The packs in `data/packs/` are not bundled with the extension: it downloads each one it needs, checked against the SHA-256 in `src/core/packs/packs.lock.json`. Their npm packages (written by `npm run data:publish`) carry the same notices in `LICENSE` and `README.md`.

## Wordnets (Chinese, Italian and Polish meanings of the terms the other sources lack)

Taken from the [Open Multilingual Wordnet 1.4](https://omwn.org/) release. The English wordnet links the pack's English terms to the other wordnets' synsets, and gives each sense's English hint (the synset's definition up to its first ";"); each other wordnet gives the native words of those synsets. Entries were selected, filtered and reformatted; Chinese words are written in simplified characters.

- [Princeton WordNet 3.0](https://wordnet.princeton.edu/): Princeton University. [WordNet 3.0 license](https://wordnet.princeton.edu/license-and-commercial-use); its full notice is in `scripts/data/licenses/wordnet-3.0.txt` and in the `LICENSE` of each package that uses it. Used in `data/packs/en-zh/`, `data/packs/en-it/`, `data/packs/en-pl/`
- [Chinese Open Wordnet](https://bond-lab.github.io/cow/): Francis Bond and Shan Wang. WordNet-style license; its full notice is in `scripts/data/licenses/chinese-open-wordnet.txt` and in the package's `LICENSE`. Used in `data/packs/en-zh/`
- [MultiWordNet](https://multiwordnet.fbk.eu/) (Italian): Emanuele Pianta, Luisa Bentivogli and Christian Girardi, Fondazione Bruno Kessler. [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Used in `data/packs/en-it/`
- [ItalWordNet](http://www.ilc.cnr.it/en/content/italwordnet): Istituto di Linguistica Computazionale, CNR. [ODC-BY 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/). Used in `data/packs/en-it/`, after MultiWordNet
- [plWordNet](https://plwordnet.pwr.wroc.pl/wordnet/): Wrocław University of Technology. WordNet-style license; its full notice is in `scripts/data/licenses/plwordnet.txt` and in the package's `LICENSE`. Used in `data/packs/en-pl/`
