- Used in: `public/packs/en/lemmas.json`, `public/packs/en/ipa.json`, `public/packs/en/pos.json`, `public/packs/en/freq.json`, `public/packs/en/phrases.json`, `public/packs/en-ja/phrases.json`, `public/packs/en-defs/`.
