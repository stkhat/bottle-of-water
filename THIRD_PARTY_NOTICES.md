# Third-party notices

Lingo uses data derived from the sources below. The files are generated by `npm run data:build`, `npm run data:es` and `npm run data:native` (scripts in `scripts/data/`). Packs in `public/packs/` are bundled with the extension; packs in `data/packs/` are bundled or downloaded as their entry in `src/core/packs/packs.lock.json` says. Their npm packages (written by `npm run data:publish`) carry the same notices in `LICENSE` and `README.md`.

## EJDict-hand (English → Japanese dictionary)

- Source: [EJDict-hand](https://github.com/kujirahand/EJDict) by kujirahand, commit `2dd9e71ffcefce89b4d946ce07bf51fa0573aac0`
- License: public domain
- Used in: `public/packs/en-ja/dict-<n>.json`, `public/packs/en-ja/capital.json`, `public/packs/en-ja/pos.json` (its senses' parts of speech), `public/packs/en/vocab.json`, `public/packs/en/phrases.json`

## Wiktionary (English inflections, pronunciations, parts of speech, word frequency, phrases, names)

- Inflections, US IPA, parts of speech, proper names (the names lexicon), phrases with their Japanese translations, English definitions of words and phrases, and the translation tables' Spanish and Portuguese words: [English Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/), extracted by [wiktextract](https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract) and published by [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/)
- Word frequency: [Wiktionary:Frequency lists/TV/2006](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Frequency_lists/TV/2006), by Wiktionary contributors
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Authors: the contributors to Wiktionary, listed in each page's history
- Used in: `public/packs/en/lemmas.json`, `public/packs/en/ipa.json`, `public/packs/en/pos.json`, `public/packs/en/freq.json`, `public/packs/en/phrases.json`, `public/packs/en-ja/phrases.json`, `public/packs/en-ja/pos.json` (the parts of speech of EJDict's senses, chosen among each word's Wiktionary parts of speech), `public/packs/en-defs/`, `data/packs/en-es/` and `data/packs/en-pt/` (with the Spanish and Portuguese Wiktionaries below). These derived files are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. They were changed from the source: entries were selected, filtered and reformatted, senses were ranked, and senses that only name another term take that term's definition; the phrase list is chosen with counts over the source's usage examples, which are not distributed
